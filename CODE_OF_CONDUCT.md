# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want this project to be a friendly, safe place for everyone who uses it or helps with it, whatever their age, background, experience or the language they speak. Questions from beginners are welcome.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient. Explain things clearly, especially to people who are new.
- Give and accept feedback politely. Criticise the code or the idea, never the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect other people's time: describe a problem with the box model, Android version and what you tried.

## What is not acceptable
- Insults, harassment, threats, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Sharing other people's private information (addresses, Wi-Fi names, passwords, IP or MAC addresses) without permission.
- Spam, advertising, or posting links to pirated apps or illegal content.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find hostile in a project like this.

## Where this applies
In the issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space that belongs to this repository.

## Enforcement
The project owner may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, open an issue that does not contain private details, or use GitHub's "Report content" tool. The owner will look at every report and keep the reporter's details private.

This code of conduct is based on the ideas of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
